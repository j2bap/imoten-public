### Autopilot-stack

**You own the stack, never the landing. Hand the operator one linear stack of base-chained PRs to review and land.** **Autopilot-full** holds except for how verified work leaves. Its operator gates, goal, hourly thread heartbeat audit, and zero-writes stop apply unchanged. Each tick re-reads both playbooks from trunk, and this one wins where they differ.

1. **Run Autopilot-full's owners, minus the landing.** Resolve the forge once through `../references/forge.md`. Owners report their code-ready head SHA, then STACK-READY with the exact head SHA in place of merge-ready. The root verifies each round per Autopilot-full step 4, and a clean verdict follows the aggregate rule in `../references/verdicts.md`. Nothing enters the stack unverified. Owners never rebase onto another branch, trunk included, and never retarget, merge, close a PR, or arm auto-merge, because the root owns the chain's topology and the operator owns the landing. When an item depends on another, its owner branches from that item's branch, not from trunk. Owners parallelize only with proven isolation.
2. **Give the topology one writer.** Owners push only their own branches and report each tip and its intended parent. Only you rebase or retarget a link, because two writers on one chain undo each other. Append each clean PR in dependency order, or an order the operator specified, with its base set to its parent's branch through the selected forge. Fetch the parent, rebase the child onto that exact tip, check the remote head with `git ls-remote`, and push with `--force-with-lease`. An owner takes your push as its new base and lease, and on a conflict resolves its own slice for you to push. Only the root PR targets trunk.
3. **Absorb drift at the root.** Until landing starts, apply Autopilot-full step 5's test to each link and rebase the chain bottom-up where it calls for one. Then apply the patch-ID rule in `playbooks/shipping.md` link by link, since a link's base is its parent. A rebase rewrites every SHA above it, so send anything no longer valid through step 1's verification again before delivery. Re-run mergeability and CI after every rewritten push, even when the patch-ID is unchanged. The countersign rule stays the same as Autopilot-full.
4. **Deliver the chain, then keep your hands off it.** The deliverable is one linear chain of verified PRs, reviewable bottom-up in the selected forge. Each link carries its verdict in its PR body or a comment. The operator reviews and lands it through their own clicks or by arming auto-merge. Once landing starts, nobody writes to the chain except the post-merge retarget that `playbooks/shipping.md` sanctions, because a second writer breaks a landing in flight. A mid-landing problem goes through you to the operator, and any fix waits for the operator's go.

**Choosing between the autopilots.** Autopilot-full when the PRs are independent and landing authority is granted. Autopilot-stack when the operator wants review before landing, the work is sequenced or coupled, or merge authority is withheld.

**Reply:** catch the operator up per the `$ketchup` skill, with links to the stack root and tip, a one-line verdict per link, and anything parked or excluded with the reason.
